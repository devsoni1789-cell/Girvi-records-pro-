How interest is calculated

Payments first clear accrued interest, then reduce principal. Each top-up starts accruing from its own date. “First and last month full” charges every calendar month touched. “First month full” charges one full month once the loan has started, then uses actual elapsed time. “Daily” calculates monthly rate × days ÷ 30. Interest is simple interest, not compound interest.
